# Privacy

SoftRing does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party libraries.
- It has no access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts, phone,
  messages, files or any other personal data. It does not know who is calling
  and does not read notifications.
- It only uses the ringtone and notification volume, alarms (to change it at
  the right time) and Do Not Disturb access (Android allows volume changes
  during a mode only to apps that have it). It plays your ringtone or
  notification sound only for a short preview when you move a slider.
- Only on Android 17 and later, it briefly starts a foreground service, because
  from that version Android ignores volume changes from an app that is not open.
  It shows no persistent notification and collects nothing.
- The only data it stores is on your device: your hours, your lowered
  volume levels and the daytime volumes to restore in the morning.
- To protect your privacy, these data are not included in phone backups nor
  copied to a new phone: after a restore SoftRing starts from scratch.
- Uninstalling the app deletes that data.
